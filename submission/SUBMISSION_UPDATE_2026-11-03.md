# Ipon Buffer — current project update

Prepared November 3, 2026. The existing submission is closed for edits; this
text has not been posted to BuilderBase.

## Short description (nine words)

Community price sharing and AI-assisted emergency savings inside e-wallets.

## Current solution

Ipon Buffer combines a photo-first community feed of local essentials prices
with a reviewed emergency-savings plan. Shoppers can share observations,
thank contributors and independently confirm prices. An optional local AI
assistant drafts expense details for review; deterministic rules calculate
contributions, and the customer confirms deposits. Simulated merchant offers
provide cashback that customers can explicitly move into the buffer.

The product is designed as a reusable feature for an existing e-wallet, with
React embedding and backend APIs. The Android APK demonstrates the experience
using a native WebView shell. It depends on the development computer for the
web app, backend and local model; it is not an install-and-forget partner SDK.

All funds, customers, merchant campaigns and reported prices are synthetic.
There is no live wallet integration, proven business uplift or real retailer
partnership. Local AI remains experimental and failed its development quality
gate. Manual entry and mandatory review remain available.
