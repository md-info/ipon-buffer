# Partner-paid service hypothesis

A wallet or financial-services partner could pay for active-user planning and validation support. No consumer fee is proposed. Adoption, willingness to pay, reduced support burden, retention effects and financial-health improvements are hypotheses. No contract, integration or commercial validation exists.

All figures below are PLACEHOLDER PHP assumptions, not vendor prices or forecasts. Per active user per month: fee 20; hosting/storage 2; support 3; partner operations 1; local inference compute 2; total variable cost 8. The compute allocation assumes 40% use AI, two assisted sessions per AI user/month, 2.5 calls/session including clarification/retry, and PHP 1 allocated compute/call: 0.4 × 2 × 2.5 × 1 = PHP 2. Hardware utilisation and latency are unmeasured. Local inference is not cost-free.

Contribution = fee − variable cost = PHP 12 per active user/month. Fixed monthly operating cost PHP 120,000 implies ceiling(120,000 / 12) = 10,000 active users at operating break-even. One-time integration PHP 300,000 is excluded; amortising over 12 months adds PHP 25,000/month and raises the illustrative threshold to 12,084. Taxes, financing and contingency are excluded.

No interest spread, commissions, insurance revenue or benefit to partner balances is counted. Discovery must establish sponsor, buyer, integration cost, procurement timeline, service obligations and evidence sufficient to justify any fee. A controlled pilot should precede a commercial impact claim.
