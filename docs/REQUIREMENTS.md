# Submission requirements and provenance

Public event page and expanded FAQs checked in the browser on September 30, 2026:
https://builderbase.com/event/asean-financial-health-challenge

Track 02 concerns accessible emergency savings fitting income and spending patterns. Format 1 requires a functional prototype, PDF deck of at most 10 slides and a working-prototype video of at most 3 minutes. A live link/test login is requested if available; a code repository is optional.

The page and FAQ state registration closes October 2, 2026, without an exact time or timezone. GFTN's announcement calls October 2 the application deadline:
https://gftn.co/press/bangko-sentral-ng-pilipinas-and-gftn-supported-by-the-unsgsa-launch-asean-challenge-to-turn-financial-health-ambition-into-scalable-solutions

Treat October 2 as the published deadline, not a verified midnight cutoff or evidence of a later submission window.

FAQ findings: solo applicants welcome; no company required; teams may be any size and must form before registration; each person may join only one team. The programme is virtual and applicants need not be in Singapore or the Philippines. This does not conclusively establish eligibility for every country or age. Applying is free and no equity is taken. The organizer FAQ describes submissions as confidential and reviewed only by the evaluation panel; this is not a review of binding legal/IP terms.

Still unverified: exact cutoff/timezone; whether registration and full submission have separate cutoffs; complete geographic/age eligibility; binding terms/IP; video delivery; upload sizes and form character limits. Authenticated application fields were not inspected. No numerical judging weights, mandatory AI or C++ requirement has been verified.
